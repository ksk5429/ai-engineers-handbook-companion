# Riverside Community Library: Volunteer Travel Reimbursement Policy
SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE for the AI Engineer's Handbook. Not a real organisation's policy.
Version 3, effective 1 March 2026. All amounts are in euros (EUR).

1. Mileage for a private car is reimbursed at 0.30 EUR per kilometre.
2. Meals are reimbursed up to 25.00 EUR per day, but only on days when travel exceeds 6 hours.
3. Hotel nights are reimbursed up to 80.00 EUR per night when a receipt is attached.
4. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the last day of the trip.
5. Alcohol is never reimbursed.
6. Items that do not fit rules 1 to 5 are referred to the treasurer for a decision.
