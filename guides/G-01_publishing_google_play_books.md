# G-01 Publishing the handbook on Google Play Books

**Guide date:** 9 Nov 2026. **Last checked against sources:** 9 Nov 2026 (R-005, R-006, R-007). **Recheck when:** Google changes Partner Center help pages, EPUBCheck releases a new major version, or the build changes. **Platform tested:** Windows 11 (build only; no upload has been made).

Publishing is the project owner's action (DECISIONS D-3). This guide lists what Google documents and what this project has verified locally.

## Steps for the owner

1. Settle the open decisions: licence (G-12), author/publisher name and the AI-assistance statement (G-13), price, genre.
2. Run EPUBCheck yourself (or allow the session to run it):
   ```text
   cd C:\Users\geolab\.local\handbook_tools\dl
   tar -xf jre.zip -C ..
   tar -xf epubcheck.zip -C ..
   ..\jdk-21.0.13+9-jre\bin\java.exe -jar ..\epubcheck-5.4.0\epubcheck.jar F:\RESEARCH\73_AI_Engineers_Handbook\dist\ai-engineers-handbook.epub
   ```
   (Folder names inside the zips may differ; use the extracted names.) A clean run ends with "No errors or warnings detected". Save the output as `dist/EPUBCHECK.txt`.
3. Sign in to Google Play Books Partner Center, add the book, upload the EPUB and the cover, fill in the Book info and Pricing tabs, and use the preview.
4. Record the outcome, including any processing errors from the Content tab, in `STATUS.md`. Only then may the project claim the book is published.
